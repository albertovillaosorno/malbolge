## Repository Use

Malbolge research retains executable artifacts, environment/configuration
identity, validation evidence, and reproducible result-generation paths. ACM
artifact-evaluation practice is useful prior art for defining what a functional,
reusable, and reproducible research artifact should expose.

## Provenance

The SIGSIM PADS artifact-evaluation guidance applies ACM artifact review and
badging concepts and describes documentation, consistency, completeness,
executability, verification/validation evidence, environment instructions, and
reproduction scripts for figures or tables.

## Evidence

### Verified

- Research artifacts can include software, models, proofs, tests, benchmarks,
  and datasets in addition to the paper itself.
- Artifact evaluation values documented, consistent, complete, exercisable
  artifacts with verification or validation evidence.
- Reproducibility guidance benefits from explicit environment instructions and
  automation of result regeneration.

### Unresolved

Malbolge does not claim any ACM badge. The repository borrows methodological
ideas and defines its own acceptance contracts.
