# Bibliography And Provenance Disclaimer

## Status

Active repository evidence boundary, reviewed 2026-07-26.

## Subject

Limits on what a record under `docs/bibliography/` means and what conclusions a
reader may draw from it.

## Repository Use

This boundary applies to every bibliography record. The bibliography catalogs
source identity and evidence so technical, research, and legal documents can
cite a shared provenance trail without silently inheriting conclusions.

## Provenance

The boundary follows the accepted bibliography taxonomy ADR and the repository
source-verification ledger. It is repository-authored governance support, not an
external source summary.

## License Or Terms

This disclaimer is repository-authored MIT material. External sources retain
their own rights and terms. Recording or citing a source never relicenses it.

## Evidence

A bibliography record may establish that a source exists, who publishes it,
which version or date was reviewed, what claims were checked, and what remains
unresolved. A bibliography record does **not** by itself:

- create repository policy;
- grant legal permission;
- prove an experimental conclusion;
- make an upstream API part of Malbolge semantics; or
- establish that a transferred research technique works on Malbolge.

Those conclusions belong to their owning technical, legal, or research records
and require the evidence declared there.
