# Bibliography And Provenance Disclaimer

`docs/bibliography/` records source identity, provenance, versions, verification
state, and repository relevance. A source record is evidence metadata, not
repository policy, an experimental conclusion, or legal permission.

`Verified` means the record established the claims listed under its verified
claims against the cited evidence as of its review date. It does not mean every
statement in the source is correct, current forever, applicable to Malbolge, or
adopted by this repository.

Negative searches and unresolved evidence are always bounded by the material
actually inspected. A missing result is not promoted to a universal absence
claim. Contradictory sources are preserved and resolved only for the specific
question their evidence can answer.

Return to the [bibliography index](index.md) or the
[repository verification ledger](provenance/repository-source-verification.md).
