## Repository Relevance

Souper is prior work for synthesis-driven compiler optimization, IR design for
superoptimization, and the use of independently checked synthesized rewrites.

## Source Quality And Provenance

The paper is a primary research source from the Souper authors.

## Verified Claims

- Souper is a synthesizing superoptimizer developed in the LLVM context.
- The paper reports using synthesis to discover optimizations and also describes
  automated optimization-pass use.
- The work demonstrates that superoptimizer IR design can transfer across more
  than one compiler environment.
