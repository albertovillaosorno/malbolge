## Repository Use

The guest `atan2` resource proof needs quantitative separation between
`tan(m)` at a nonzero rational dyadic midpoint and a reduced rational input
ratio. The publication specifically treats `tan(r)` for nonzero rational `r`
and states irrationality measure 2, making it a substantially more specialized
candidate than a general exponential-transcendence measure.

The result is retained only as asymptotic number-theory evidence. Irrationality
measure 2 does not by itself give the repository a concrete denominator
threshold or uniform constant for all reduced denominators through the proved
adaptive ceiling `q < 2^106`.

The publisher search metadata states that the paper uses generalized continued
fractions to approximate `e^r`, `tan(r)`, and `tanh(r)` for nonzero rational
`r`, and states that their irrationality measures are 2. The publicly rendered
article page also states the generalized-continued-fraction method and measure
2, but an effective lower-bound constant or finite denominator threshold was
not exposed in the reviewed metadata. This record therefore does not infer one.

### Unresolved

The reviewed publisher material does not expose a verified effective threshold
`q_0(r)` or a concrete positive constant `C(r)` such that every rational `p/q`
beyond that threshold satisfies a lower bound suitable for guest-C resource
planning. Consequently the statement that the irrationality measure is 2 is
not a finite correct-rounding resource bound and does not make public `atan2`
available.
