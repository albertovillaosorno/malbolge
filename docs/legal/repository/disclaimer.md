# Legal Research Disclaimer

## Status

Active repository legal-research boundary.

## As-of Date

2026-07-26.

## Question Presented

What may a reader infer from material under `docs/legal/`, and which conclusions
remain outside the authority of this repository?

## Verified Baseline

Material under `docs/legal/` is maintained for repository-boundary analysis,
source-use research, licensing traceability, and interoperability planning. It
is not legal advice, a compliance certification, or authorization to copy,
modify, circumvent, distribute, publish, or monetize third-party material.

## Not Established

A result for a particular person, source copy, jurisdiction, agreement,
generated artifact, or distribution event depends on facts that this repository
cannot establish globally. Public availability, technical interoperability,
successful parsing, independent implementation, or an accepted repository ADR
does not determine a legal outcome by itself.

## Required Facts

A consequential source-use or distribution decision must identify at least:

- the exact source and version involved;
- the applicable license, agreement, or other authority;
- the relevant jurisdiction and actor when material;
- the transformation and generated artifact at issue; and
- the publication, distribution, circumvention, or commercial act proposed.

## Authorities

Primary legal authorities and authoritative license texts should be preferred
where the repository needs a legal proposition. Canonical source identity and
provenance belong under `docs/bibliography/`; legal interpretation remains here.

## Analysis

Legal records distinguish verified source facts from engineering assumptions and
unresolved application questions. Technical capability is evidence that an
operation can be performed, not evidence that a person is legally permitted to
perform or distribute the result.

Consequential publication, distribution, circumvention, or commercial decisions
should be checked against current primary authorities and advice applicable to
the exact facts.

## Conclusion Boundary

Legal documentation provides bounded repository research only. It does not
replace advice from qualified counsel for a concrete legal decision.
