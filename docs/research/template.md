# Research Record Template

- Status: Proposed
- Research ID: `{stable-id}`

## Question

State one bounded research question.

## Hypotheses

Record falsifiable hypotheses before interpreting results where practical.

## Prior Work

Cite bibliography records and identify the baseline techniques being compared.

## Method

Define workloads, algorithms, controls, seeds, resource budgets, stopping rules,
and verifier/oracle behavior.

## Correctness Boundary

State how correctness is established independently from optimization quality.

## Measurements

Define primary and secondary metrics before results are interpreted.

## Results

Retain positive, negative, and null outcomes with raw-output provenance.

## Threats To Validity

State limitations, confounders, generalization limits, and unresolved evidence.

## Conclusion

State only conclusions supported by the recorded evidence.
